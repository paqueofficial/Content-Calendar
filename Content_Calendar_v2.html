Content Calendar

Plan your posts on a calendar — a line on what's going out each day, and the screenshot or thumbnail of the video right there with it.

How it works

  1. Name your project. Move through the months with the arrows, or jump back to now with Today.
  2. Tap any day to add what you're posting — a short description, the platforms it's going to (tick as many as you like), a time if you know when it goes out, and best of all a screenshot or thumbnail of the clip itself. New entries start as Planned.
  3. Add several posts to the same day and they line up in time order, earliest first, so a busy day stays clear. Mark entries posted as you go with the toggle beside each one. Days with a thumbnail show it right on the calendar, so the whole month reads as a visual plan at a glance.
  4. Save the session as a file (.json) to keep or reopen anywhere — your images travel with it. The page also auto-saves to this browser, but that's not a real backup.
