Content Studio

Take content from first idea to posted — brainstorm and rate ideas up top, then schedule the ready ones onto a calendar you can open any time.

How it works

  1. Plan up top. Add an idea in your own words, name the track it's for, pick a format, and rate it for effort × reach. Set where it is: idea, to shoot, shot or edited. Choosing To shoot opens an optional date, so you know when you're filming it.
  2. Sort to decide. Sort by pipeline order, by what's urgent, by shoot date, or quick wins first — the low-effort, high-reach ideas.
  3. Schedule when ready. Hit Schedule on an idea, pick the date it goes out, tweak the description and add a screenshot or thumbnail, then submit to calendar. The idea gets an "On calendar" tag so you know it's locked in.
  4. View calendar any time from the button at the bottom — with or without content — to see your week or month at a glance. Add times and platforms, mark things posted, and print the month.
  5. Save the session as a file (.json) to keep or reopen anywhere — images travel with it. The page also auto-saves to this browser, but that's not a real backup.
